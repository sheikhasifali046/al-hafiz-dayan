About the Shop

Wear Your Best Persona

Al Hafiz & Dayan is a bespoke tailoring house in Okara — a place where good fabric, honest workmanship and a perfect fit come first. From everyday shalwar kameez to wedding sherwanis, every garment is cut and stitched by hand, to your measurements, your style and your occasion.

We believe a well-stitched suit does more than fit — it carries your personality. That is why every order is measured, cut and finished with the same care, whether it is a simple kurta or a full three-piece suit.

“Quality Craftsmanship, Timeless Style.”
